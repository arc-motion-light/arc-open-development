# Code of conduct

ARC seeks respectful, technically rigorous collaboration among people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Explain evidence, critique designs rather than people, acknowledge uncertainty and give credit for others' work.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, deliberate disclosure of personal information, and repeated disruption are unacceptable. Respect privacy and confidential information. Do not post operational secrets when discussing a defect.

Report concerns privately to [juan@arcstore.io](mailto:juan@arcstore.io). Include relevant context without unnecessarily sharing personal data. ARC will review available information and may request clarification, remove inappropriate content or restrict participation. No response deadline or independent appeals body is promised by this initial policy. The founder currently manages enforcement; governance can be updated as the initiative grows.

This policy covers future ARC-managed collaboration spaces and conduct representing the initiative. It is original policy text, not a claim of adopting another project's standard conduct agreement.
