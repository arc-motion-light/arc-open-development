# Governance

ARC Open Development is founder-led. Juan-Antonio Søren Espinoza Pedersen, trading as ARC Motion & Light, currently approves publication, licensing decisions, contributor-rights arrangements and commercial commitments.

Maintainers may prepare changes and record test evidence, but publication authority must be explicitly delegated before anyone acts on ARC's behalf. No board, foundation, elected committee or independent legal organization is implied by the GitHub organization.

Engineering proposals should state the problem, alternatives, safety and ownership boundaries, evidence, remaining uncertainty and migration implications. Record accepted architectural changes in provenance documentation rather than presenting every experiment as a release.

Separate technical maturity from repository publication state. A compiled image may still lack hardware acceptance; a technically mature private repository may still be legally or operationally unready for publication.

Policies evolve through reviewed changes. Licensing an already released work and licensing future revisions are distinct decisions; avoid promises that all historical grants can be withdrawn. External contribution acceptance remains limited under [CONTRIBUTING](CONTRIBUTING.md).
