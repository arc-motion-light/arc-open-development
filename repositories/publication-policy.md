# Publication policy and states

| State | Meaning | Required transition evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Planned | Repository proposed | Scope and source owner identified |
| In preparation | Local audit/documentation work active | Audit inventory and evidence collected |
| Under review | Prepared package awaiting review | Rights, security, build and documentation findings available |
| Ready for publication | Reviewed scope and founder approval recorded | Exact candidate commit approved; unresolved blockers closed |
| Published | Approved content actually made public | Public location and published commit/release recorded |
| Archived | No longer maintained as an active publication | Reason, successor and support limits recorded |

These states describe publication, not functionality. Technical maturity must be recorded separately.

Each firmware release needs ownership and third-party review, full-history security inspection, provenance preservation, reproducible build evidence, honest hardware limits and visible commercial contact. Do not rewrite original private history to prepare a public tree. Keep sanitized distributions separately and record their relationship privately and in a safe public summary.

The founder approves the exact candidate and any subsequent push or visibility change. Approval of Phase 1 documentation is not automatic approval to publish every firmware repository. Use [the preparation process](../publication/repository-preparation.md) and [approval record](../publication/founder-approval.md).
