| External drivers/libraries/generated code | Inventory each | To verify | To verify | To verify | Pending | Pending |

For each dependency, retain applicable license text and attribution, identify modifications, inspect binary redistribution conditions and distinguish generated code from original authorship. ARC's firmware license does not replace upstream licenses.

Exclude unresolved components from a candidate or resolve their permissions before approval. Do not copy entire vendor trees merely because they are in a private build. See [licensing checklist](../publication/licensing-checklist.md).
