# Licensing review checklist

For each candidate repository:

- [ ] Establish ownership for original files and supported copyright years.
- [ ] Identify generated, vendor, contributed and mixed-origin files.
- [ ] Inventory each dependency with version/source, license, notices and redistribution obligations.
- [ ] Confirm permission to redistribute binary SDKs and generated/vendor material; do not assume.
- [ ] Use the complete unmodified PolyForm text only for eligible ARC-owned firmware.
- [ ] Preserve all applicable third-party licenses and attribution.
- [ ] Apply approved CC BY-NC 4.0 to original public documentation with attribution; exclude executable tooling, third-party material, hardware and branding.
- [ ] Verify official license texts and retain PolyForm unmodified.
- [ ] Review logo provenance, faithful transformations and reserved branding rights; avoid registered-trademark claims without evidence.
- [ ] Preserve the scoped MIT grant for the validator; resolve other tooling, hardware and non-brand image licensing separately.
- [ ] Establish contributor rights sufficient for the intended public/commercial uses.
- [ ] Check compatibility and record unresolved issues without speculative conclusions.
- [ ] Obtain founder approval for the exact public scope.

ST HAL, STM32Cube/STM32WPAN, OpenThread, CMSIS, drivers and libraries are audit categories, not one license. The [third-party notice template](../templates/third-party-notices.md) must be populated from actual components. This checklist grants no additional software restrictions or rights.
