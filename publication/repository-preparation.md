# Future repository preparation procedure

1. Identify application scope, exact private source, responsible rights holder and proposed publication name.
2. Preserve the original history and evidence privately. Prepare a separate working distribution; do not restructure operational development trees as a shortcut.
3. Inventory original/generated/vendor/contributed content. Record licenses, notices and redistribution constraints before copying source.
4. Audit current files, all proposed history, tags, large-file objects, build outputs and attachments for secrets and confidential content, including internal review reports. Ignoring or deleting a tracked file does not remove its historical copies.
5. Select public scope and record any sanitation/mapping. Do not publish raw backups or personal development paths.
6. Reproduce target builds and appropriate tests in an audited working environment. Separate hardware observations from host simulations.
7. Complete README, copyright, third-party notices, security/contribution policy and commercial contact. Apply the selected license only to eligible material.
8. Review the exact candidate, record founder approval and only then establish publication/remotes in a separately authorized task.

Phase 1 does not perform steps 2–8 for firmware. Use the [repository registry](../repositories/repository-registry.md) to track progress rather than implying publication from a local preparation directory.
