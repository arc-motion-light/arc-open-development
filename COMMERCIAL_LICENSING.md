# Commercial licensing

ARC welcomes discussions about OEM manufacturing, embedded firmware integration, product-development partnerships, industrial applications, joint research and development, technology licensing and commercial distribution.

Contact **[juan@arcstore.io](mailto:juan@arcstore.io)** with the intended application, software or product family, deployment context and requested commercial scope. Avoid sending confidential designs or credentials in an initial message; agree a suitable exchange process first.

A commercial agreement can address uses beyond the applicable PolyForm firmware or CC BY-NC documentation permissions. Branding and hardware are separate scopes. Contacting ARC is not itself a license, purchase order or commitment to supply. Existing public permissions remain governed by their license; this policy does not narrow them.

Pricing, royalties, sublicensing, exclusivity, support, warranties and delivery commitments are not standardized in this repository. They require separate written agreement with the relevant rights holder. Third-party rights cannot be granted by ARC beyond ARC's actual authority.
