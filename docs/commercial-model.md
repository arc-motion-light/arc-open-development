# Commercial development model

ARC intends to build products and technology partnerships around embedded control, sensing and connectivity. Public engineering documentation can help potential collaborators assess design choices before discussing an integration.

Potential applications include intelligent lighting for people, plants and built environments; environmental monitoring; technical education; industrial sensing/control; and motion systems. These are application directions, not a catalog of market-ready or certified products.

The selected source-available firmware policy supports permitted experimentation while leaving uses outside the license's scope to separate commercial agreement. Hardware, branding and documentation decisions remain distinct. Third-party software permissions must remain intact.

For OEM integration, manufacturing, joint R&D or commercial distribution, use [the commercial licensing contact](../COMMERCIAL_LICENSING.md). No standard price, royalty, exclusivity or service promise is established here. The business is currently a Danish personally owned enterprise; potential incorporation and any IP transfer need formal arrangements.
