## Approved scope

The founder approved **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International**, SPDX `CC-BY-NC-4.0`, for original ARC-owned public documentation on 8 October 2026. In this repository that covers original Markdown documentation, explanatory asset metadata, policy prose, issue/PR templates and documentation templates, including original explanatory diagrams. The [complete legal code](licenses/CC-BY-NC-4.0.txt) is copied accurately from Creative Commons.

Retain supplied attribution/license notices, identify modifications and link the license when sharing covered material. The terms permit noncommercial sharing and adaptation; copyright is retained, not transferred. Commercial uses outside the license's permissions require separate agreement. [Official license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/) · [legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/legalcode.en).

These documentation permissions do not extend to executable scripts, branding, hardware/manufacturing files or third-party material. Their inclusion beside licensed prose does not change their terms. Approved documentation reuse is not conditional on obtaining ARC's publication approval; the review gate governs ARC's own releases.

## Firmware policy

ARC-owned firmware is designated for **PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0**, SPDX `PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0`. Its [official complete text](licenses/PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0.txt) remains unmodified. No existing firmware is copied or relicensed in this task.

The license permits uses, changes and distribution within its terms. Its personal and named organizational permissions must not be reduced to a blanket ban on all commercial organizations. Uses beyond those permissions require a separate arrangement. This is **source-available**, not OSI-approved open source. No additional ARC restrictions are appended to the standard license. [Official text](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0).

## Material-by-material matrix

| Material | License or policy | Scope / remaining work |
|---|---|---|
| Original ARC firmware | PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 | Apply only to verified ARC-owned paths in separately reviewed firmware distributions |
| Original ARC public documentation and prose templates | CC BY-NC 4.0 | Approved; original Markdown and explanatory diagrams in this repository, with exclusions below |
| ARC trademarks, logos and branding assets | All rights reserved | `assets/branding/` images and staged profile images excluded from both licenses |
| Hardware schematics, PCB layouts, manufacturing files and mechanical CAD | Separate licensing decision | No hardware grant or distribution made here |
| Third-party software and documentation, including official license texts | Original applicable terms | Preserve upstream rights/notices; ARC cannot relicense them |
| Software tooling and executable scripts | Separate licensing decision | `tools/*.py` excluded; no broader reuse license adopted for these scripts |
| Photographs and non-brand illustrations not established as original documentation | Separate rights review | Determine ownership and scope before import |
| External contributions | Reviewed contributor arrangement required | Ownership does not automatically transfer to ARC |

The documentation license grants no trademark or patent rights. Source publication does not create registered patent protection. Copyright in hardware drawings is not a general monopoly on functionally similar circuits.
